# Sketch 001: Design System

## Design Question
Which of three distinct visual identities best conveys the trustworthy, enterprise-grade feel SmartQueue needs for hospitals, clinics, and government offices?

## How to View
Open `.planning/sketches/001-design-system/index.html` in a browser.

## Variants
- **A: Neutral Enterprise** — #0f172a dark sidebar, slate palette, blue (#2563eb) primary. Closest to Linear. Familiar to enterprise users, high contrast, information-dense.
- **B: Clean Light** — Pure white background, indigo (#5469d4) accent, Stripe-style meticulous spacing. Feels professional and approachable — good for patient-facing screens.
- **C: Deep Professional** — Near-black (#09090b), emerald (#10b981) accent, monospace data. Vercel aesthetic. Technical and precise — resonates with operators who live in dashboards.

## What to Look For
1. **Sidebar treatment** — Does dark-on-dark (A/C) or light sidebar (B) feel right for a daily-use admin tool?
2. **Typography weight** — Do the number displays in stat cards feel readable enough for glanceable dashboards?
3. **Badge legibility** — Are the WAITING/SERVING/COMPLETED status badges clear at a glance?
4. **Data density** — Does the terminal-style mono data in C feel like a feature or a barrier?
5. **Overall trust signal** — Which would you trust at a hospital reception desk?
